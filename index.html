Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

How are the hieroglyphs chosen for my name?
Each letter in your name is mapped to its closest phonetic hieroglyph — the same system ancient Egyptians used to write foreign names inside cartouches. For example, the letter "A" becomes the Egyptian vulture glyph (𓄿).
What materials are the pendants made from?
Our gold cartouches are crafted from 18K gold-plated sterling silver, and our silver cartouches are polished 925 sterling silver. Both are hypoallergenic and designed to last.
How long does it take to make and ship?
Each pendant is handcrafted to order. Production takes 3–5 business days, and shipping typically takes 5–10 business days depending on your location. We ship worldwide.
Does the pendant size change with the name length?
Yes! The plate grows vertically to fit longer names. Each glyph stays the same size — we never shrink them. The preview on our site shows you the exact proportions before you order.
Can I return or exchange my cartouche?
Since each pendant is custom-made with your name, we cannot accept returns for change of mind. However, if there's a defect or error, we'll happily remake it at no extra cost. Contact us within 14 days of delivery.
Do you offer gift wrapping?
Every cartouche ships in a premium velvet pouch inside a branded gift box — no extra charge. It's ready to gift straight out of the package.

Our story

In ancient Egypt, a cartouche wrapped a royal name in a protective oval rope — a symbol of eternal protection reserved for pharaohs and gods. The cartouche told the world: this name matters.

Today, we bring that tradition to everyone. Your name, transliterated into the authentic phonetic sounds of hieroglyphs, raised in polished metal on a pendant that grows to fit every letter.

  • Raised reliefGlyphs stand out from the plate like a stamped pendant, catching light beautifully.
  • Made to fitLonger names get a longer plate — we never shrink glyphs.
  • Gold & silver18K gold-plated or 925 sterling silver, polished by hand.
  • Authentic phoneticsWe use the same transliteration system as Egyptologists.

The heritage of cartouches

4,000 years of history in your hands

c. 2600 BCE — The Old Kingdom

The cartouche first appears during the reign of Pharaoh Sneferu. Originally an oval rope with a knot, it symbolized everything the sun encircles — infinite protection.

c. 1350 BCE — Tutankhamun's era

Cartouches reached their artistic peak during the New Kingdom. Tutankhamun's cartouche, found in his tomb, remains one of the most recognized symbols of ancient Egypt.

1799 — The Rosetta Stone

Jean-François Champollion used the cartouches on the Rosetta Stone as the key to deciphering hieroglyphs, recognizing that they contained royal names.

Today — Your name, your cartouche

We continue this tradition with modern craftsmanship. Each pendant is handmade using the same phonetic principles that ancient scribes used thousands of years ago.
